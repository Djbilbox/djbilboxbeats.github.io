How I work

Every beat starts in FL Studio and stays there through arrangement and mix, so what you hear in the preview is what you get in the file. Drums are layered by hand rather than pulled from a preset chain, and each track is mixed to sit correctly under a vocal instead of being mastered loud and left flat.

Oriental production is the part I am known for. Rather than approximating maqam scales on a western keyboard, I sample real oud, qanun, ney and darbuka and tune them in quarter tones — the same engine I later turned into a plug-in.

Questions

What does a beatmaker do?

A beatmaker composes and produces the instrumental a song is built on — drums, bass, melody, arrangement and mix. Here every beat is written in FL Studio, mixed in-house, and delivered as MP3, WAV or stems depending on the licence.

Can I use these beats for free?

Yes. More than 1000 instrumentals are free for profit: release them on streaming platforms as long as you credit DJBILBOX BEATS. Paid leases drop the credit requirement and add WAV files and track stems.

Which genres do you produce?

Mainly hip-hop, trap, drill, afrotrap, G-funk, deep house and oriental. The oriental side uses real maqam quarter-tone tuning, not a western-scale approximation.

Do you make custom beats?

Yes. Custom production and exclusive rights are available on request — by email at djbilboxbeats@gmail.com or through the Discord server.
