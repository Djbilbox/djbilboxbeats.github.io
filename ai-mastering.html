The chain

What actually happens to your track.

No black box. Three stages, all of them visible on the interface, all of them yours to override.

Input Stereo metering with L/R peak, and an input trim so the processor sees the level it expects.
Auto Gain Reads your material and sets the make-up so the loudness lands where it should — the "AI" part of the job.
Auto Comp Attack, release and threshold adapt to the density of the mix instead of a fixed preset.
Gain reduction A live meter in dB — you see how hard it's working, so you can back off before it squashes.
Harmonics Adds weight and glue on the way out. Dial it to taste, from transparent to coloured.
Output & dither True-peak ceiling, peak hold, and 16/24-bit dither for the final bounce.
